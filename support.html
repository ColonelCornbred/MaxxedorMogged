❓ Frequently Asked Questions

My photo has been pending for a long time — what's happening?

All photos are reviewed by our moderation team before going live. This usually takes less than 24 hours. If your photo has been pending for more than 48 hours, email us and we'll look into it.

My photo was rejected — why?

Photos are rejected if they contain explicit content, appear to feature someone under 18, are AI-generated, or violate our community guidelines. If you believe your photo was rejected in error, contact us.

How do I delete my photo?

Go to your Profile screen, find the photo you want to remove, and tap the 🗑 Delete Photo button at the bottom of the photo card.

I hit my daily vote limit — how do I get more votes?

Free users get 100 votes per day. You can watch a rewarded ad for +50 bonus votes, or upgrade to the Unlimited plan for unlimited daily votes.

How do I restore my Unlimited subscription?

Open the app and go to the paywall screen. Tap "Restore Purchases" at the bottom. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my Unlimited subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Maxxed or Mogged → Cancel. On Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → Maxxed or Mogged → Cancel.

Can other users contact me or see my personal info?

No. Maxxed or Mogged has no messaging feature and no personal information is ever displayed publicly. Your email and identity are completely private — only your photos and vote stats are visible.

How do I report a photo?

Tap the ⚑ Report button in the top right corner of the voting screen. Select your reason and submit. Reported photos are automatically hidden pending review.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at MaxxedorMogged@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Account" and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.
