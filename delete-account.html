📱 Delete From the App

The easiest way to delete your account is directly within the app:

1
Open the Maxxed or Mogged app and log in
2
Tap your profile icon to go to your Profile screen
3
Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
4
Confirm deletion when prompted — your account will be permanently deleted immediately

✉️ Delete by Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us:

MaxxedorMogged@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 30 days.

🗂️ What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your profile information (name, date of birth, gender)
  • All photos you have uploaded
  • Your vote history
  • Your push notification tokens

⚠️ What Is Retained

The following data may be retained after account deletion:

  • Aggregated, anonymized voting statistics that cannot be linked back to you
  • Records required for legal or regulatory compliance for up to 90 days
  • Subscription transaction records retained by Apple App Store or Google Play as required by their policies

💳 Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription. To avoid future charges, cancel your subscription before deleting your account:

  • Android: Google Play → Subscriptions → Maxxed or Mogged → Cancel
  • iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Maxxed or Mogged → Cancel
